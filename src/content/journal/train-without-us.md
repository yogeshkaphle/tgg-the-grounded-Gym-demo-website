Here is something gyms don't like to say out loud: most people who stop training don't stop because they are lazy. They stop because life moved. A work trip to Biratnagar. Two weeks of exams. Monsoon evenings when the road outside floods. And every year, Dashain and Tihar, when half the city goes home and nobody's routine survives the travel, the food and the family.

Three weeks later, they feel guilty about coming back. Six weeks later, they don't.

So we decided to do something that sounds strange for a gym. We teach you to train without us.

## Base camp, not the summit

I guided treks for three seasons before I started coaching. On a trek, base camp is not where the adventure happens. It's where you rest, eat, check your gear, and get advice from people who have done the climb before. Then you go out and do the hard part yourself.

That is how we think about the gym. It's where you learn to squat and swing properly. Where a coach watches your form and adds weight when you are ready. Where you meet people who make you want to turn up at 6:30 in the morning.

But the training that keeps you ready happens everywhere. In a hotel room in Pokhara. In the park near your in-laws' house. In your own living room when it is pouring outside.

## What "train anywhere" actually looks like

It isn't a vague promise to "stay active". It's four specific sessions, written by our coaches:

- The [Park Session](/train-anywhere/park), for a bench and a bar.
- The [Hotel Room Session](/train-anywhere/hotel-room), quiet enough for the room below.
- The [Monsoon Evening Session](/train-anywhere/home), with a backpack and a bag of rice.
- The [Any-Gym Session](/train-anywhere/gym), for a corner and a pair of dumbbells.

And we teach them in the gym. The Anywhere Circuit class runs the same movements, so the first time you do a split squat off a hotel bed, it isn't the first time you've done a split squat. Your coach has already fixed your knee position.

## Isn't this bad for business?

People ask. Our answer: the members who stay longest are not the ones who never miss a class. They are the ones who miss a week and don't lose the habit. A gym that keeps you training through Dashain keeps you as a member in Mangsir.

It also keeps us honest about what fitness is for. If your strength only works under our roof, it isn't really the kind we're after.

## Before your next trip

Save the [Hotel Room Session](/train-anywhere/hotel-room) on your phone. Pack a light backpack. Do it on your first evening away, before the routine has a chance to slip. It takes 25 minutes, and it will make your first class back feel like coming home instead of starting again.
